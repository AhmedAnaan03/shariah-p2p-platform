# PB-16, PB-17, PB-31 Marketplace, campaign page and Shariah-only mode

Sprint 4 · stories · 3 + 3 + 2 points · traces to FR2, FR3, FR9, NFR3 · GitHub issues #16, #17, #31

Built as one item: all three work on the same two investor pages and the same query. PB-16
lists campaigns, PB-17 opens one, and PB-31 decides which of them an investor may list, open
and pledge to. See `docs/decisions.md`, 25 Nov 2026.

Screenshots for the report are taken once in Sprint 5 (PB-56), so there are none here.

## What was built

### PB-16 Browse, search and filter the marketplace

- **Card grid.** The marketplace's table is now a grid of `CampaignCard`s: three across on a
  wide screen, two on a tablet or narrow desktop, one on a phone. Each card shows:
  - the contract badge, with the Shariah badge for Murabahah and Mudarabah;
  - the risk grade, or "Not yet scored" until PB-25 scores campaigns;
  - the title, then business, sector and purpose (an individual is "Individual borrower");
  - target, the return figure and tenure;
  - funding progress, investor count and days left.

  The label above the return figure follows the contract: Interest, Markup or Profit share. The
  terms are three aligned columns of label, figure and unit ("8.5%" / "a year", "7.5%" /
  "in total", "60 : 40" / "investors : business"), so a Murabahah markup can't be read as a yearly
  rate, and a figure never breaks across lines on a narrow card.
- **Six filters on the shared `ListFilters` bar (PB-54)**, all applied on the server:
  - **Sector:** the 12 SME sectors, plus "Individual borrower".
  - **Purpose:** start-up, growth, education, personal.
  - **Amount:** a two-handle range slider (Radix Slider) from MVR 10,000 to 2,000,000 along a
    stepped, roughly logarithmic scale (10K, 15K, 20K … 750K, 1M … 2M), so small amounts aren't
    squeezed into one end. It filters when a handle is released (after a 300 ms pause for key
    presses), keeps `amount_min` / `amount_max` in the URL and has a Reset. While a handle moves, a
    tooltip above it shows the exact amount ("MVR 250,000"). Arrows move one step, Page Up / Down
    ten; each handle reads its amount in MVR (`aria-valuetext`). The server accepts whole numbers
    only, clamps them to the request limits and swaps a reversed pair.
  - **Tenure:** up to 12 months; 13 to 36; over 36.
  - **Contract (the "Shariah mode" filter):** conventional, Murabahah, Mudarabah, or any
    Shariah contract.
  - **Risk grade:** A to E, or "Not yet scored". It reads the campaign's latest successful risk
    score, so pending or failed attempts don't count.
- **Keyword search** on the title and the business name (never an individual's name), with the
  300 ms debounce.
- **Pagination.** Nine cards to a page.
  - Page links keep the search and filters.
  - Changing a filter goes back to page 1.
  - A page past the end goes to the last page.
  - Everything is in the URL, so a filtered page can be bookmarked and the campaign page's back
    link returns to it.
- **New components:** `CampaignCard`, `ProgressBar`, `RiskGrade`, `Pagination` and `RangeSlider`.

### PB-17 Campaign detail page with disclosures

`/investor/campaigns/{id}` shows, for any open campaign:

- **Purpose and business background:** what the money is for, and the business background.
- **Borrower:** borrowing as (individual or SME). For an SME it adds the business name, sector
  and years operating. It never shows an individual's name, contact details, address, ID or
  documents.
- **Terms:**
  - amount (the asset cost for a Murabahah) and tenure;
  - the contract type, with its `Term` explanation and the Shariah disclaimer on Shariah
    campaigns;
  - the contract's own terms.
- **Projected return:**
  - **Conventional:** the yearly interest rate.
  - **Murabahah:** the markup as a total percentage of the asset cost for the whole tenure, never
    a yearly rate ("8.0% markup over 12 months"), with a note that says so.
  - **Mudarabah:** the investors' share of the actual profit, with no figure. Its return depends
    on profit not yet made.
- **Repayment history on Amanah:** how the borrower has paid the instalments of their earlier
  funded campaigns. It counts instalments due so far as paid on time, paid late or overdue now,
  plus campaigns funded and fully repaid, in one plain sentence. A first-time borrower gets
  "No repayment history on Amanah yet".
- **Credit history:** the borrower's three self-declared answers, from the request's snapshot.
- **Funding:** a `FundingRing` with raised against target and the investor count. It shows 0%
  until pledging exists (PB-19). The ring fills from 0 on first view (instantly with reduced
  motion).
- **Risk grade (reserved):** a labelled `RiskGradeCard` in its "Not yet scored" state and an
  empty "Why this grade" panel, which PB-25 and PB-26 fill. If a score exists, its grade,
  estimated chance of missed repayments and model version are shown already.
- **Pledge status and risk warning:** why pledging is locked, or that it opens in a later
  release, and the risk warning in Shariah-safe words.
- **Navigation:** the back link and breadcrumb return to the marketplace page it was opened
  from, with its filters.

A campaign that isn't open (draft, waiting, rejected, suspended, past its deadline) answers 404.

**Telling borrowers (added at the developer's request).** The onboarding Finances step and the
financing request form now say that investors will see the borrower's credit history answers and
repayment history on the campaign page. They also say what investors never see: an individual's
name (shown as "Individual borrower"), income figures, address, ID number or documents. The text
comes from one place, `InvestorDisclosure`, and the new `DisclosureNotice` component shows it.

### PB-31 Shariah-only marketplace mode for investors

The top bar's Shariah-only switch already saved the choice per investor (PB-51). Now it changes
what the investor can reach, and every check is on the server:

- **Marketplace:** only Murabahah and Mudarabah campaigns are listed. The contract filter offers
  only those two, and `?contract=conventional` in the URL is ignored. A search can't reach a
  hidden campaign either. The page says Shariah-only is on and how to turn it off.
- **Campaign page:** opening a conventional campaign by its address sends the investor back to the
  marketplace with an information message explaining why. This also happens if the switch is
  turned on while that page is open.
- **Pledging:** the `pledge` gate now takes the campaign. It refuses a campaign that isn't open,
  and a conventional campaign for a Shariah-only investor. PB-19 attaches it to the pledge route
  as `->can('pledge', 'campaign')`, so hiding the card is never the only protection.

## Design decisions and alternatives

- **Tenure as preset ranges, amount as a slider.** Tenure has few values, so a dropdown of ranges
  fits the shared bar and needs no validation; a range includes its upper bound only. The amount
  spans two orders of magnitude, so it is a two-handle slider on a stepped, roughly logarithmic
  scale. On a linear scale, most requests (under MVR 100,000) would share the first 5% of the
  track. The server still checks it: whole numbers only, clamped, reversed pairs swapped.
- **Sidebar positions independent of the animating width.** The bottom row is laid out at a
  fixed width from the left edge, so the positions Motion measures for its layout animation are
  the final ones even while the CSS width transition is still running. Tooltips on the nav items
  are always mounted (just disabled when expanded), so collapsing never re-creates a link and
  restarts its fade.
- **One query decides visibility.** `Campaign::visibleTo($investor)` is used for the list, the
  "is anything open" check and (through `browse`) the campaign page. Hiding only in React was
  rejected: the data would still be sent, and a typed URL or a pledge request would get past it.
- **Redirect, not 403, for a hidden campaign.** The app has no styled error pages, and the
  redirect tells the investor why and how to see it. The policy refusal carries its own code,
  `hidden_by_shariah_only`, so the controller can tell it apart from "not found" without parsing
  a message.
- **Murabahah markup as a total.** A markup is fixed for the whole tenure. Shown per year it
  would look like an interest rate, which is what the contract exists to avoid. The card says
  "in total" and the page says "markup over N months", and both are tested.
- **Raised and investors are counted, not stored.** `withSum` and `withCount` read the pledges
  table, which is empty until PB-19, so the page is correct today and needs no change later.
- **Risk grade filter on the latest successful score.** A `HasOne ... ofMany` relation picks the
  newest `scored` row. A failed re-score therefore doesn't remove a campaign's grade.
- **Repayment history from the schedule table.** Only instalments already due count (future ones
  say nothing about how a borrower pays). Dates are compared in Maldives time, so an instalment
  paid at 23:00 in Malé on its due date is on time.

## Limitations

- No campaign has a risk grade until PB-25, so the grade filter only ever finds "Not yet scored".
- Funding is 0% everywhere until pledging exists (PB-19).
- Sorting is fixed to the closest deadline first.

## Sample code

The server decides what a Shariah-only investor can reach. The same scope is used for listing,
counting and opening, so no path skips it (`web/app/Models/Campaign.php`):

```php
public function scopeVisibleTo(Builder $query, User $investor): void
{
    $query->listed()->when(
        $investor->investorProfile?->shariah_only,
        fn (Builder $q) => $q->whereIn('contract_type', self::SHARIAH_CONTRACTS),
    );
}
```

The campaign page turns the policy's coded refusal into a friendly redirect, and any other
refusal into a 404 (`web/app/Http/Controllers/Investor/CampaignController.php`):

```php
$access = Gate::inspect('browse', $campaign);
if ($access->code() === CampaignPolicy::HIDDEN_BY_SHARIAH_ONLY) {
    $request->session()->reflash();

    return to_route('investor.campaigns.index')->with('notice', $access->message());
}
$access->authorize(); // 404 for a campaign that isn't open
```
